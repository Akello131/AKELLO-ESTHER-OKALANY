Who We Are

Engineering the Future

Project Forge is a student engineering collective at Kyambogo University. We are four founding members united by a single belief: that engineering should solve real problems, not just pass exams.

We build practical skills through hands-on projects, documenting every step — every failure, every breakthrough — and sharing it openly. Our community grows as our impact does.

We are non-profit, community-driven, and operating in the Engineering Services space. Every project we take on is aimed at delivering measurable, lasting value.

The Solar Maximizer

Our debut project tackles one of East Africa's most pressing challenges — unreliable electricity. The Solar Maximizer is designed to optimize solar energy capture and storage, making clean power more accessible and efficient for homes and small businesses.

We are building, testing, and iterating in the open — learning by doing, and documenting everything so others can learn too.

  • Optimized solar energy harvesting for local conditions
  • Affordable design using locally sourced components
  • Scalable from household to community level
  • Open-source documentation and build logs
